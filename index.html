301 Darts Scorer · STM32 Breadboard Prototype

Darts Scorer, 1 to 4 Players Blue Pill, dual I2C seven-segment displays, LCD1602, keypad, PWM audio

An STM32F103C8T6 Blue Pill scores 301 or 501 for one to four players. Two HT16K33 backpacks show the scores, an LCD1602 behind a 74HCT541 buffer shows names and status, a 4×4 keypad takes input, and a PAM8403 amplifier plays the callouts.

This page is the bring-up sequence. It walks 28 steps in nine stages, one subsystem at a time, and lights the exact pins for the step in progress. Each stage ends in a measured pass criterion before the next subsystem is connected.

    Design traps in this build
    

      

        
HCT, not HC

        
The LCD needs 5 V logic and the Blue Pill drives 3.3 V. The 74HCT541 has a TTL-compatible input
        threshold near 2 V, so it reads a 3.3 V high correctly and outputs a full 5 V level. A plain 74HC541
        powered from 5 V needs about 3.5 V for a guaranteed high, which the MCU does not supply. Read the
        marking on the part before seating it.

      

      

        
Two 3.3 V regulators, never joined

        
The regulator on the Blue Pill supplies the MCU from USB. The AMS1117 module supplies the 3.3 V
        rail for the displays from the 5 V rail. The Blue Pill 3V3 pin stays off the 3.3 V rail. It feeds
        the brightness pot only. The ST-Link carries SWDIO, SWCLK and GND, and its 3.3 V pin stays open.

      

      

        
USB power limits

        
The 5 V rail is VBUS from the Blue Pill 5V pin through F1, a 1 A resettable fuse. A computer
        USB 2.0 port supplies 500 mA. Worst-case draw is estimated at 480 to 650 mA, so use a source rated
        1 A or more. VBUS can fall toward 4.4 V at the board through a thin cable, and the AMS1117 needs
        about 1.1 V of headroom. Measure both rails with the displays at full segments.

      

      

        
I2C address collision

        
Both backpacks ship at 0x70. Bridge the A0 jumper on display 2 before it joins the bus, which
        moves it to 0x71. Two devices at one address both acknowledge, and the scan cannot tell them apart.

      

      

        
PAM8403 outputs are bridge-tied

        
Each speaker terminal is driven. Neither output terminal connects to ground, and the two channels
        are not joined at the output. The mono source is split at the input through two 10 kΩ resistors.

      

      

        
Amplifier and speaker ratings differ

        
The PAM8403 is rated 3 W per channel and the speaker 0.5 W. Firmware caps the PWM duty so the
        speaker is never driven past its rating. Volume is a firmware scale factor. There is no hardware
        volume control.

      

      

        
Audio filter corner

        
fc = 1 / (2πRC) = 1 / (2π × 1 kΩ × 0.1 µF) ≈ 1.6 kHz

        
Starting values. The coupling capacitor after the filter must be 0.1 to 1 µF. A capacitor
        marked 102 is 1000 pF, which passes almost no audio.

    If something is wrong
    

      
ST-Link cannot connect

      
Check SWDIO and SWCLK are not swapped, GND is common, and the ST-Link 3.3 V pin is open.
      If firmware set SYS Debug to No Debug, hold reset while connecting, then reflash with Serial Wire enabled.

      
I2C scan finds nothing

      
Check SDA is on PB7 and SCL on PB6, not reversed. Measure both lines idle: each must sit near 3.3 V.
      A line stuck low points to a short or a reversed backpack supply. Confirm the scan passes the address shifted left by one bit.

      
Scan finds one address with both displays connected

      
The A0 jumper on display 2 is not bridged, so both sit at 0x70.

      
LCD shows a row of solid blocks

      
Power and contrast are correct and the controller is not initialized. Check the six data lines through
      the buffer in order, and the 4-bit init sequence timing.

      
LCD is blank with the backlight on

      
Turn the contrast pot through its full travel first. Then confirm VDD is the 5 V rail and RW is grounded.

      
Keypad column 3, column 4 or row 4 never responds

      
PB3, PB4 and PA15 are JTAG pins at reset. They become GPIO only with SYS Debug set to Serial Wire.

      
Several keys register per press

      
Row and column leads are assigned to the wrong connector pins. Map the keypad connector with a
      continuity meter before trusting any published pin order.

      
Green or blue LED channel is dim

      
Their forward voltage is close to the 3.3 V pin drive level. Reduce those two resistors to 100 to 150 Ω. Leave red unchanged.

      
Audio is silent or very faint

      
Check the coupling capacitor value, then confirm PWM on PB8 with a meter on AC volts. Confirm the
      amplifier has 5 V and that no speaker terminal touches ground.

      
Displays flicker or the MCU resets during audio

      
The 5 V rail is sagging under amplifier load. Measure the rail during a tone and lower the firmware duty cap.
